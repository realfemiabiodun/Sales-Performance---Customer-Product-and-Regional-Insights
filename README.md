# Sales-Performance Customer-Product-and-Regional-Insights

## Excel-based Sales Performance Dashboard analyzing customer, product and regional insights, developed during the Easy Technologies Academy Data Analytics Internship.

## Project Overviews
The project is an interactives Sales Performance Dashboard built on Microsoft Excel to uncover meaningful insights from the sales data and communicate them through an interactives Excel dashboard. It address business performance, customer behaviour, and regional trend using visual storytelling to support data-driven decisions.

## Business Questions
This dashboards helps answer key business questions by providing insights from sales performance data:

1. What is the overall sales performance in terms of total revenue, total quantity, total orders, Avergae Order Value (AOV) and Average Unit Price?
2. How is sales performance changing over time across months and years?
3. Which regions are generating the highest and lowest revenue?
4. Which products contribute most to total revenue?
5. Who are the top customers driving sales performance?
6. How does revenue vary across customer segments such as age group and gender?

## Problem Statement
The business lacks a clear and consolidated view of its sales performance across customers, products, and regions. Sales data exists, but it is not structured in a way that makes it easy to track performance trends, compare year-on-year growth, or identify key revenue drivers and underperforming segments. Without an interactive and centralized dashboard, decision-makers struggle to understand current performance, monitor growth over time, and focus on areas that require strategic attention.

## Dashbord Overview
The dashboard was developed to transform two years of raw sales data into a clear, interactive, and decision-focused analytical tool. The process began with preparing and modeling the dataset using Power Pivot to establish accurate relationships across customers, products, and regions. Key business metrics were then created using DAX to measure revenue, orders, quantities, average order value, average unit price, and year-on-year performance.

Using PivotTables and PivotCharts, sales trends were analyzed across time, regions, products, and customer segments. These insights were consolidated into an interactive Excel dashboard with filters controls, enabling users to explore performance dynamically. The final dashboard provides a comprehensive view of current performance, performance trends over time, and areas requiring strategic attention, supporting informed and data-driven decision-making.

## Tools Used
1. Microsoft Excel: Used as the primary analysis and visualization platform for building the interactive sales dashboard.
2. Power Pivot: Used for data modeling, managing relationships between tables, and handling large datasets efficiently.
3. DAX (Data Analysis Expressions): Used to create calculated measures such as Total Revenue, Total Orders, Average Order Value (AOV), Average Unit Price, and Year-on-Year (YoY) growth metrics.
4. PivotTables: Used to summarize, aggregate, and analyze sales data across different dimensions such as time, region, product, and customer segments.
5. PivotCharts: Used to visualize trends, comparisons, and performance patterns in a clear and interactive manner.
6. Excel Slicers: Used to enable dynamic filtering and interactive exploration of the dashboard.

## Key Insights

1. Revenue More Than Doubled — Growth Is Demand-Driven
Total Revenue reached 14.54M, with a 114.6% YoY increase. Revenue grew from 6.77M in 2023 to 7.76M in 2024, showing steady and sustained expansion.
Growth was primarily volume-driven:
- Quantity increased by 112.3%
- Orders increased by 103.7%
- Average Unit Price grew only 1.1%

Decision Insight
Growth is fueled by increased customer demand rather than aggressive price increases. The business should prioritize scaling operations, inventory planning, and customer acquisition strategies to sustain volume-led growth.

 2. Customer Spending Power Improved in 2024
Average Order Value increased from 2,761 (2023) to 3,049 (2024). This indicates customers are spending more per transaction in 2024, strengthening overall revenue quality.

Decision Insight
The 2024 product mix or pricing strategy is more effective. The business should analyze high-value product bundles and replicate successful upselling strategies.

3. Product Demand Shift Signals Changing Consumer Preferences
Product leadership changed:
2023: Toaster – 0.17M
2024: Yoga Mat – 0.21M
Yoga Mat was not dominant in overall performance but gained strong traction in 2024.

Decision Insight
Customer preferences are evolving. The company should invest more in trending or emerging product categories and monitor demand shifts early to stay competitive.

4. Regional Revenue Concentration with Expansion Opportunity
The West region remained dominant:
2023: 2.41M
2024: 2.82M
However, the North region improved from 0.38M to 0.42M, showing early signs of growth.

Decision Insight
While the West drives performance, geographic revenue concentration presents risk. Strategic marketing and distribution expansion in underperforming regions like North could unlock additional growth.

5. Balanced Product Contribution Reduces Risk
Top overall product: Vacuum Cleaner – 0.43M
Lowest product: Flour – 0.34M
The relatively narrow revenue gap suggests no extreme overdependence on a single product.

Decision Insight
The product portfolio is relatively balanced, reducing concentration risk and supporting sustainable revenue distribution.

## Recommendations

1. Scale Volume-Driven Growth
With revenue increasing by 114.6% YoY, primarily driven by order (+103.7%) and quantity growth (+112.3%), the business is clearly experiencing demand-led expansion.

Recommendation
Invest in inventory optimization, supply chain efficiency, and customer acquisition strategies to sustain high order volume. Since pricing contributed minimally (+1.1%), scaling operations is more critical than price adjustments.

2. Leverage High AOV Momentum
Average Order Value increased from 2,761 in 2023 to 3,049 in 2024, indicating stronger customer spending behavior.

Recommendation
Identify high-performing product bundles or premium products contributing to higher AOV and replicate these strategies across other categories. Introduce upselling and cross-selling campaigns to further increase transaction value.

3. Invest in Emerging Product Trends
The shift from Toaster (2023 leader) to Yoga Mat (2024 leader) signals evolving consumer demand.

Recommendation
Monitor fast-growing product categories and allocate more marketing and inventory investment toward emerging high-growth items. Establish a quarterly product performance review to detect shifts early.

4. Reduce Regional Revenue Concentration Risk
The West region dominates revenue, while North shows gradual improvement (0.38M → 0.42M).

Recommendation
Launch targeted regional campaigns in underperforming areas to diversify revenue streams. Expanding geographic contribution reduces dependency risk and supports sustainable growth.

5. Maintain Balanced Product Portfolio
The narrow gap between top and lowest-performing products suggests healthy distribution across the portfolio.

Recommendation
Continue diversifying product offerings while avoiding overreliance on single-product revenue streams. Maintain portfolio balance to protect against demand volatility.
